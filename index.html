Hello.

Who?

You, your bike, and anyone who wants a long, social ride with frequent ice cream stops.

What?

A 34-mile bike ride to seven ice cream shops in Manhattan, Queens, and Brooklyn. The mileage is intentional. This is a social ride, not a race.

When?

Saturday, date to be announced. Meet at 11:00 am, roll at 11:30 am, and expect to finish around 6:00 pm.

Where?

Meet at Lost Borough, 4791 Broadway in Inwood.

From there, we will ride to six more stops:

  1. Lost Borough at 4791 Broadway, Manhattan — mile 0
  2. Softside at 51 Spring Street, Manhattan — mile 11.4
  3. The Screen Door at 48-19 Skillman Avenue, Queens — mile 16.7
  4. Common Meadows at 227 Grand Street, Brooklyn — mile 20.4
  5. Dolly's Coffee Shop at 53 Rockaway Avenue, Brooklyn — mile 24.9
  6. L'Albero dei Gelati at 341 Fifth Avenue, Brooklyn — mile 29.6
  7. Julia Jean's at 202 Clinton Street, Manhattan — mile 33.9

See the ice cream list on Google Maps.

Why?

Because New York is fun to cross by bike, ice cream is good ride fuel, and the long way gives us time to talk.

How?

By bike. Electric or acoustic is fine. This will be a no-drop ride, with regrouping after major turns and bridge crossings.

Bring a helmet, lock, lights, water, payment, and a spare tube. Share or sample at the stops if seven full servings sounds like too much.
