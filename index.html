bikecream

Hello.

Who?

You, your bike, and anyone who wants to enjoy a long, social ride with filled with ice cream!

What?

A 34-mile bike ride between seven ice cream shops in Manhattan, Queens, and Brooklyn.

Many of the shops are pulled from NYTimes' Best Ice Cream Shops in New York City list.

When?

Sunday, September 13th! Meet at 11:00 am at the Lost Borough and expect to finish around 6:00 pm.

Google Calendar user? Join here (optional).

Where?

Meet at Lost Borough, 4791 Broadway in Inwood.

From there, we will ride to six more stops:

  1. Lost Borough at 4791 Broadway, Manhattan — mile 0
  2. Softside at 1231 Broadway, New York, NY 10001 — mile 11.4
  3. The Screen Door at 48-19 Skillman Avenue, Queens — mile 16.7
  4. Common Meadows at 227 Grand Street, Brooklyn — mile 20.4
  5. Dolly's Coffee Shop at 53 Rockaway Avenue, Brooklyn — mile 24.9
  6. L'Albero dei Gelati at 341 Fifth Avenue, Brooklyn — mile 29.6
  7. Julia Jean's at 202 Clinton Street, Manhattan — mile 33.9

Why?

Why not?! New York is a great bike city and ice cream is delicious. What is not to love?!

How?

By bike! Electric, acoustic, unicycle, or whatever you want is good! This will be a no-drop ride and I will do my best to make sure we stick together!

Bring a helmet, water, and money. Share with others if seven scoops sounds like too much.
