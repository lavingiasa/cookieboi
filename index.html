03 / RIDE THE RIDE

Long ride.
Soft rules.

This is a social ride, not a race. We move together and make room for every kind of comfortable city bike.

01

No-drop means no-drop.

We regroup after major turns and bridge crossings. Nobody gets abandoned with melting ice cream.

02

Electric or acoustic.

Ride the bicycle you trust for 34 city miles. No mopeds, obviously.

03

Share the scoop.

Seven full desserts is a dare, not a requirement. Split, sample, hydrate, repeat.

04

Bring the basics.

Helmet, lock, lights, water, payment, a spare tube, and a little patience for the group.
